About Stevox Consulting Inc.

Technology decisions should improve the business, not add complexity.

Stevox Consulting Inc. is an Ottawa based consulting company focused on data engineering, analytics, architecture, governance, and AI readiness.

We work with organizations that need to modernize fragmented data environments, improve reliability, strengthen governance, or establish better foundations for analytics and emerging AI use cases.

Our approach combines hands on engineering with architecture and advisory. The objective is not simply to introduce more technology, but to create data environments that are understandable, maintainable, secure, and useful to the business.

Leadership

Senior technical leadership grounded in delivery.

Stevox is led by Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc., whose background combines enterprise data engineering, analytics, cloud architecture, governance, regulated data, and responsible AI.

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc.

Founder and Principal Consultant

Senior data and analytics professional with more than 12 years of experience across engineering and technology.

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc., is a senior data and analytics engineer with more than 12 years of professional experience across engineering and technology, including more than 8 years focused on data engineering and analytics.

His experience spans enterprise data platforms, cloud architecture, data modeling, analytics delivery, governance, security, healthcare data, financial data, and large scale modernization initiatives.

Stephen holds a Doctor of Science in Information Technology. His doctoral research and publications focus on algorithmic transparency, fairness, explainable artificial intelligence, and bias detection in automated employment decision systems.

Stephen also serves as a Board Director with the Canadian Information Processing Society (CIPS) Ontario, contributing to governance policy, organizational oversight, and professional information technology standards. At the national level, he serves on the CIPS Certification Review Committee, evaluating professional certification applications against Skills Framework for the Information Age (SFIA) competency and professional practice standards.

Professional profile

Stephen combines hands on technical delivery with architecture, governance, and advisory experience. His work is centered on building reliable data foundations, improving analytics, strengthening controls, and helping organizations prepare responsibly for AI enabled use cases.
