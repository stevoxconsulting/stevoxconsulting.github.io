About Stevox

A specialized technology consultancy built around data.

Stevox Consulting Inc. is an Ottawa based consulting company focused on data engineering, analytics, architecture, governance, and AI readiness.

We work with organizations that need to modernize fragmented data environments, improve reliability, strengthen governance, or establish better foundations for analytics and emerging AI use cases.

Our approach combines hands on engineering with architecture and advisory. The objective is not simply to introduce more technology, but to create data environments that are understandable, maintainable, secure, and useful to the business.

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc.

Founder and Principal Consultant

Senior data and analytics professional with experience spanning enterprise engineering, analytics, cloud architecture, governance, and responsible AI.

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc., is a senior data and analytics engineer with more than 12 years of professional experience across engineering and technology, including more than 8 years focused on data engineering and analytics.

His experience includes enterprise data platforms, cloud architecture, data modeling, analytics delivery, governance, security, healthcare data, financial data, and large scale modernization initiatives.

Stephen holds a Doctor of Science in Information Technology. His doctoral research and publications focus on algorithmic transparency, fairness, explainable artificial intelligence, and bias detection in automated employment decision systems.

He also contributes to professional technology governance and standards through leadership and service with CIPS Ontario and CIPS National.
