Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc.

Founder and Principal Consultant.

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc., is the Founder and Principal Consultant of Stevox Consulting Inc. and a senior data and analytics professional with more than 12 years of experience across engineering and technology, including more than 8 years focused on data engineering and analytics. His experience spans enterprise data platforms, cloud architecture, data modeling, analytics delivery, governance, security, healthcare data, financial data, and large scale modernization initiatives. He combines hands on engineering with architecture and advisory, with a focus on building reliable data foundations and translating technical decisions into measurable business outcomes.

Stephen holds a Doctor of Science in Information Technology. His doctoral research and publications focus on algorithmic transparency, fairness, explainable artificial intelligence, and bias detection in automated employment decision systems. He also serves as a Board Director with the Canadian Information Processing Society (CIPS) Ontario, contributing to board governance, organizational oversight, and professional information technology standards, and serves on the CIPS National Certification Review Committee, where he evaluates professional certification applications against the Skills Framework for the Information Age (SFIA) and established professional practice standards.
