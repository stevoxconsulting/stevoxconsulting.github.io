Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc. Founder and Principal Consultant
Professional background

Stephen Fabeyo, D.Sc., is a senior technology, data, and analytics leader with 12+ years of experience spanning engineering, enterprise technology, and digital transformation, including 8+ years specializing in data engineering and analytics. His work encompasses enterprise data platforms, cloud and data architecture, data modeling, analytics, governance, security, and large scale modernization initiatives across complex healthcare, financial, and enterprise environments. He brings together hands on engineering expertise, architectural leadership, and strategic advisory to design resilient technology foundations, guide transformation initiatives, strengthen data capabilities, and translate complex technical decisions into measurable organizational outcomes.

He holds a Doctor of Science in Information Technology, with doctoral research focused on responsible and trustworthy artificial intelligence, including algorithmic transparency, fairness, explainable AI, and bias detection in automated employment decision systems. His academic work complements his industry experience by bringing a research informed perspective to emerging questions of AI governance, accountability, and responsible technology adoption.

Beyond his consulting and technology leadership, Stephen contributes to the advancement of the Canadian technology profession through governance and professional service. He serves as a Board Director with the Canadian Information Processing Society (CIPS) Ontario, contributing to organizational governance, strategic oversight, and the advancement of professional standards within the technology community. He also serves on the CIPS National Certification Review Committee, contributing to the assessment and recognition of information technology professionals across Canada.
